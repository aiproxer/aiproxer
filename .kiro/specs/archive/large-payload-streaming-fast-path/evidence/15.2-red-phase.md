## 1. Mutations Applied (RED Phase)

To demonstrate that the test assertions actively catch deviations, missing wiring, and disabled wire proofs rather than passing vacuously, two targeted mutations were introduced:

1. **Wire Proof Attachment Bypass (`internal/plugins/backends/openaicompat/backend.go`)**:
   `attachWireProof` wiring on `execbackend.Backend` was bypassed in `NewBackend` by returning `be` directly without attaching `ResolveWireRequest`, `ResolveWireDomain`, or `OpenWire`.
2. **Credential Handling Bypass (`internal/plugins/backends/openaicompat/wire_open.go`)**:
   In `WireOpenPrimitives.OpenWire`, credential acquisition from `p.Pool` was removed (leaving `cred.Secret` empty) and error cooldown / auth invalid marking via `p.Pool.MarkRateLimited` and `p.Pool.MarkAuthInvalid` was removed.

The mutations produced the expected concrete failures across both capability resolution and wire execution:
1. `TestResponsesWireRequest_ExactCompatibility` failed because `be.ResolveWireRequest` was nil when `attachWireProof` was not attached.
2. `TestResponsesWireDomain_UniversalAndRestricted` failed because `be.ResolveWireDomain` was nil.
3. `TestResponsesWireRequest_StaticModelInventoryRestriction` failed with `reason: unsupported` because `ResolveWireRequest` was unpopulated on the backend.
4. `TestOpenWire_BackendIntegration` and `TestOpenWire_NoAuthCompatibleBackendSucceedsWithoutAuthorizationHeader` failed because `be.OpenWire` was nil.
5. `TestOpenWire_CredentialCooldownAndRecoverableError` failed because rate-limit error classification did not mark cooldown on the credential in `p.Pool`.
6. `TestOpenWire_SuccessStreaming` failed because credential secret was not acquired or propagated to the outbound `Authorization` header (`receivedAuth = "", want Bearer sk-wire-success`).
